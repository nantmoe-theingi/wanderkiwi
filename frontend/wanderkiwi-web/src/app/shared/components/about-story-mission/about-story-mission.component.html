OUR STORY

Made for explorers,
by New Zealand lovers.

Wander Kiwi was born from a passion for Aotearoa's breathtaking landscapes and vibrant culture.

We noticed planning a trip can be overwhelming and time-consuming. That's why we built Wander Kiwi - to make trip planning easy, personalised, and inspiring using the power of AI and local insights.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower travelers to explore New Zealand confidently and meaningfully with AI-powered recommendations and authentic local knowledge.

Our Vision
